# Bounty Hunter Agreement — Proposed §6.6 Residual Compensation (Redline Package)

> **Note:** This is contract drafting, not legal advice. Have counsel review this language before execution.

Source document: `Aethyx_Bounty_Hunter_Agreement.docx` — *Bounty Hunter Disclosure & Independent Contractor Agreement*, Section 6 (Commission Structure).

**Numbering decision (resolved):** the clause is inserted as **6.6**, appended after the existing 6.5. The existing numbering is left completely untouched — no renumbering, no conforming cross-reference edits anywhere else in the document. Appending is strictly lower-risk than renumbering: a renumber would require auditing every cross-reference in the agreement for staleness, and the only thing gained is that residual sits adjacent to the other rate clauses. Placing it after the *Net Collected Service Fees* definition also reads naturally, since the residual clause leans on that definition.

**Rate lock (resolved):** the 5% rate locks at the referred client's signature for the full Residual Window. Aethyx retains the right to *increase* it, but not to reduce it mid-window. See 6.6(a) and the Section 17 carve-out in Part 3.

---

## 1. New Clause — §6.6 Residual Compensation

**Insertion instruction:** Insert the following as new **6.6**, immediately after the existing **6.5 Net Collected Service Fees**. No other clause is renumbered.

> **6.6 Residual Compensation.** For a Qualifying Referral that becomes a paying Aethyx client, the Bounty Hunter may earn a residual commission of 5% of Net Collected Service Fees on each additional purchase that client makes from Aethyx during the Residual Window, including additional service packages, add-ons, care plans, retainers, and other recurring or one-time service purchases.
>
> (a) **Residual Window; Rate Locked at Signature.** The "Residual Window" is the twelve (12) month period beginning on the date the referred client signs Aethyx's applicable service agreement. The Residual Window runs from that signature date regardless of whether any website or other deliverable is launched, delivered, or completed, and regardless of whether any milestone under 6.1 is earned. The residual rate in effect on the date that signature occurs is fixed for the entire Residual Window for that client. Notwithstanding Section 17, Aethyx will not reduce the residual rate applicable to an open Residual Window; Aethyx may increase it, and any increase applies prospectively from its stated effective date.
>
> (b) **Single Rate; Replaces and Does Not Stack.** The 5% residual rate replaces rather than stacks with the rates in 6.2 and 6.3 for any purchase that falls within the Residual Window. Only one commission rate applies to any given transaction. Eligibility for the growth rate under 6.3 does not increase the residual rate, which remains 5%.
>
> (c) **Original Engagement Excluded.** The engagement that first converted the Qualifying Referral into a paying Aethyx client is not residual-eligible and is compensated solely under 6.1 or 6.2, as Aethyx classifies it in writing.
>
> (d) **Bundled Plans.** Where a purchase is sold as a single bundled plan or package that includes hosting, domains, software, licenses, or similar items that Net Collected Service Fees would otherwise exclude, the residual is calculated on the full plan price actually received and retained by Aethyx. This subsection is an express and intentional exception to 6.5 for residual commissions only, and does not change how 6.5 applies to commissions under 6.1, 6.2, or 6.3. Taxes, financing fees, and amounts collected on behalf of a third party and remitted to that third party remain excluded.
>
> (e) **Earned Only on Amounts Received and Retained.** A residual is earned only on amounts Aethyx actually receives and retains. If a payment is reversed, refunded, credited, disputed, fraudulent, or charged back, the related residual may be cancelled, recalculated, or offset in accordance with Section 8.
>
> (f) **Automatic Expiration; No Tail.** Residual eligibility ends automatically when the Residual Window closes. Residuals do not renew, do not vest, do not extend by reason of client renewal, and do not continue after the Residual Window regardless of the client's continued relationship with Aethyx. Consistent with Section 14, residuals connected to questioned conduct may be paused during a reasonable investigation, and residuals may be denied or forfeited, and stop accruing, upon suspension or termination for cause under Section 14, to the extent permitted by law. Undisputed residuals already earned before termination remain payable, subject to lawful offsets and required documentation.

## 2. Conforming Amendment — §6.1

**Amendment instruction:** Replace the first sentence of **6.1 Full Website Builds** with the following. The remainder of 6.1 is unchanged.

> **6.1 Full Website Builds.** For a project Aethyx classifies in writing as a full website build, the Bounty Hunter may earn up to $200 in milestone compensation: (a) $100 when the referred client signs Aethyx's applicable service agreement, and (b) $100 when the resulting website is launched. The $200 figure is a cap on milestone compensation for that build only and does not limit, offset, or reduce residual commissions earned under 6.6.

## 3. Conforming Amendment — §17 (Program Changes)

**Amendment instruction:** Add the following sentence to the end of **Section 17**.

> Notwithstanding the foregoing, a change to the residual rate under 6.6 will not reduce the residual rate applicable to any Residual Window that is already open on the effective date of the change. Aethyx may increase the residual rate at any time, effective prospectively.

*Rationale:* without this, Section 17's protection for commissions "already earned" arguably leaves a future in-window purchase unprotected, meaning the 5% could be cut mid-window. Belt-and-suspenders with 6.6(a) — either alone would likely do the work, but a rate promise that a general amendment clause can quietly undo is the kind of term that erodes trust in a referral program precisely when it is working.

## 4. Plain English Summary

*For sharing with Bounty Hunters — not part of the Agreement.*

- If a client you referred buys anything else from Aethyx in the **12 months after they sign** their first service agreement, you earn **5%** of what Aethyx collects on it — new packages, add-ons, care plans, retainers.
- The clock starts at **signature**, not at launch. Even if the site never goes live, your 12 months still runs.
- **Your 5% is locked in** the day your client signs. Aethyx can raise it, but cannot lower it on a client who has already signed.
- **5% is the residual rate, period.** It replaces the 6% and 10% rates for anything bought inside that 12-month window — the rates never stack, and hitting the growth tier doesn't raise it.
- The **first project** that got the client in the door isn't a residual. That one pays under the full-build ($200) or smaller-package (6%) terms.
- On **bundled care plans**, you get 5% of the whole plan price, even the hosting portion. If the client gets refunded or charges back, the matching residual comes back off. Residuals stop when the 12 months ends — there's no renewal or trailing payment.

## 5. Drafting Notes

**A. Numbering — RESOLVED.** Clause is **6.6**, appended after the existing 6.5. No renumbering. All internal cross-references in 6.6(d) point to **6.5** (Net Collected Service Fees), which is its correct existing number. No stale references remain.

**B. Section 17 — RESOLVED.** The rate is expressly locked for an open Residual Window, in both 6.6(a) and a Section 17 carve-out. The lock is one-way: Aethyx may raise the rate, never lower it mid-window.

**C. "Referred Client" is not a defined term.** The Agreement defines "Qualifying Referral" (Section 5), "Bounty Hunter," and "Aethyx," but never capitalizes or defines "Referred Client" — 6.1 uses lowercase "referred client." I have therefore used lowercase "referred client" and anchored eligibility to "a Qualifying Referral that becomes a paying Aethyx client," which matches the phrasing already in 6.3. If you want "Referred Client" as a capitalized defined term, it must be added to Section 5 as a genuine definition.

**D. "Residual Window" is a new defined term.** Introduced and defined in-line in 6.6(a), following the Agreement's own pattern (6.5 defines Net Collected Service Fees in-line; Section 5 defines Qualifying Referral in-line).

**E. Reversal language lives in Section 8, not Section 6.** Section 6.5 only *excludes* refunds and chargebacks from the fee base; the operative reversal, recalculation, and offset mechanics are in **Section 8 (Cancellations, Refunds, Chargebacks, and Adjustments)**. 6.6(e) cross-references Section 8 rather than restating it, so the two cannot drift apart.

**F. Bundled-plan carve-out is a margin question.** 6.6(d) expands the residual base to the full bundled price, but hosting revenue in a bundle is frequently offset by a pass-through cost Aethyx pays a third party. As drafted, Aethyx pays 5% on gross bundled revenue and absorbs that spread. This is the stated intent. Carve-outs are limited to taxes, financing fees, and true agency/remittance amounts. To narrow it to *care plans specifically*, replace "sold as a single bundled plan or package" with "sold as a care plan or other single bundled plan."

**G. Interaction with §6.3's "three Qualifying Referrals" counter.** 6.3 grants the growth tier "after three Qualifying Referrals become paying Aethyx clients." It is not stated whether residual-generating repeat purchases by an *existing* referred client count toward that three. They should not — the counter is about distinct referrals — but the Agreement does not say so expressly. Consider adding to 6.3: "Repeat purchases by an existing referred client do not count toward this threshold." Not included above because it edits 6.3, which was outside scope.

**H. Multiple agreements by the same client.** If a referred client signs a *second* full service agreement during an open Residual Window, this draft treats it as an additional purchase inside the existing window at 5% (per 6.6(b)) rather than opening a second, later-expiring window. If Aethyx would rather have a new agreement restart or extend the window, say so expressly — the current draft resolves it against the Bounty Hunter.

**I. Milestone-cap language.** The 6.1 amendment says "up to $200 in milestone compensation" and adds an express non-limitation sentence. Belt and suspenders, deliberately — the added sentence alone would do the work, but the inserted words "in milestone compensation" close off the reading that $200 is a per-referral ceiling.

**J. Dual-role participants — RESOLVED.** Aethyx now runs a single referral program. Clients are auto-enrolled in the Bounty program on creation and earn the identical scale (6.1, 6.2, 6.3, 6.6). The former client-only scale ($200 first reward + $150 completion bonus) is retired. There is therefore no rate conflict for a person who is both a client and a Bounty Hunter, and no possibility of one referral triggering two commission structures.

**Consequence for this Agreement:** clients now hold a referral link without having signed this Agreement. That is fine for *holding* a link, but a client who actually earns a commission is being paid as an independent contractor, which means the W-9 requirement (Section 7) and the tax-reporting obligations apply to them exactly as they do to an applicant Bounty Hunter. Recommend either (i) requiring a client to accept this Agreement before their first commission is released, or (ii) adding a short client-referrer addendum covering W-9, disclosure obligations, and the no-authority-to-quote terms. Payout should remain gated on W-9 either way. This is a live gap, not a drafting nicety — it is the point where an auto-enrolled client stops being a customer and starts being a payee.
